# Business Requirements

## Problem

Analysts need timely access to trusted market data. Manual reporting creates delays and quality risks.

## Requirements

- Ingest market, weather and macroeconomic datasets.
- Transform raw data into Bronze, Silver and Gold layers.
- Create analytics-ready market metrics.
- Validate data quality.
- Provide dashboard-ready outputs.
- Track freshness, latency and quality.
- Document deployment using CI/CD and Terraform.
